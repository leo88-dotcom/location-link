How booking works

Pick your dates

Choose a flat, put in your dates and how many are coming. You’ll see the full price before you do anything else.

Book on Airbnb

Every flat page has a button straight to its Airbnb listing, with the same flat and the same dates. Booking directly on this site is coming soon.

Check in yourself

Check-in is self-service from 3pm with a lockbox, so a late train doesn’t matter. The code and directions come a few days before you arrive.

Ten flats, one owner

Every flat is looked after by the same owner, not an agency, so the standard is the same whichever you pick. Longer stays of a week or a month are welcome.

Groups of more than six: the two Spitalfields lofts are next door to each other, and the Shoreditch flats are a few minutes apart. Ask, and we’ll work out the split.
