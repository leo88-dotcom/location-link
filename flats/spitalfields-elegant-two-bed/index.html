About the flat

Stay in the heart of vibrant Spitalfields in this larger-than-average two-bedroom, two-bathroom apartment, featuring a spacious open-plan living and dining area. Bright, modern, and thoughtfully designed for comfort, the apartment is ideal for families, groups, couples, and business travelers. With Spitalfields Market, Shoreditch, Liverpool Street Station, restaurants, cafés, and shops all just moments away, it offers an excellent base for exploring Central London.

Bedrooms

The apartment features two comfortable and well-proportioned bedrooms.

The master bedroom features a king-size bed and a private ensuite bathroom with both a bathtub and shower.

The second bedroom features a comfortable double bed, making the apartment ideal for families, friends, couples, or colleagues travelling together.

Living Area

The spacious living room offers plenty of room to relax, featuring two comfortable three-seater sofas, a coffee table, and a 55-inch TV with Netflix. Large windows allow plenty of natural light into the apartment, creating a bright and inviting atmosphere throughout the day.

The living room flows seamlessly into the dining area, providing a comfortable shared space for meals, conversations, or simply relaxing together.

Kitchen & Dining

The fully equipped kitchen has everything you need to prepare meals during your stay.

The adjacent dining table comfortably seats up to six guests, making it ideal for enjoying breakfast together, having dinner after a day in London, or working remotely.

You'll also receive complimentary Nespresso coffee and tea upon arrival.

Bathrooms

The apartment has two full bathrooms, offering added convenience for groups and families.

The master bedroom benefits from a private ensuite bathroom with both a bathtub and shower, while the second full bathroom is easily accessible from the rest of the apartment.

Fresh towels and toiletries are provided for your stay.

Explore Spitalfields & East London

The apartment puts you in an excellent position to experience some of London's most vibrant neighborhoods.

Spitalfields Market is right on your doorstep, offering an exciting mix of food, independent shops, boutiques, and cafés.

Shoreditch and Brick Lane are also within easy reach, known for their street art, restaurants, vintage shops, nightlife, and creative atmosphere.

Liverpool Street Station is approximately a 3-minute walk away, providing excellent connections across London and beyond.

Getting Around

Liverpool Street Station & Underground – approximately 3 minutes' walk. Enjoy convenient access to the London Underground and National Rail services, making it easy to explore London.

The surrounding area is also highly walkable, with plenty of restaurants, cafés, shops, markets, and attractions within easy reach.
